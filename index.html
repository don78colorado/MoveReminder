Move Reminder helps you build a more active routine by checking your step count and reminding you to move when you have not reached your hourly step goal. It can also show your step progress throughout the day.

How does it work?

Move Reminder reads step data from Health Connect. You can use steps recorded by your phone, a watch, or another app that shares steps with Health Connect.

  • You set the number of steps you want to take each hour.
  • The app checks your steps at minute 50 of each eligible hour.
  • If you have not reached your hourly goal, the app sends a reminder about ten minutes before the hour ends.
  • You can customize your hourly goal and active hours in the app’s settings.

Step data may take time to appear in Health Connect, especially when it comes from another app or a watch.

Does this app need to stay open to work?

No. Scheduled reminders can work while the app is closed if Health Connect background step access, notifications, and Alarms & reminders access are allowed. These permissions can be reviewed in the app or your phone’s settings.

Do I need to keep the phone with me?

If you rely on your phone to count steps, carry it with you while walking. You can also use steps from a watch or another app if it shares them with Health Connect.

Will this app work if my device does not have a step count sensor?

It may. Move Reminder can use steps shared with Health Connect by another device or app. Phone step counting depends on your device and Android version. Check that step data appears in Health Connect and in Move Reminder.

What is 'Smart mode'?

Smart mode allows reminders only after you have had an active hour that day. This can help avoid reminders before you have started moving. With Smart mode off, reminders can begin at the hour set by the 'Daily starting hour' setting.

Why am I not receiving reminders when I should be?

Check the following:

  • In Health Connect, make sure Move Reminder has permission to read steps.
  • For reminders while the app is closed, allow background step access if your device offers it.
  • Make sure notifications are allowed for Move Reminder.
  • On Android 12 and later, allow Move Reminder under Alarms & reminders so it can check your steps on schedule.
  • Confirm that the hourly goal, active hours, and Smart mode settings allow a reminder at that time.
  • If your phone has battery optimization or protected-app settings, try allowing Move Reminder to run in the background.
  • If your steps come from a watch or another app, check that it is sharing steps with Health Connect. Syncing may take time.
