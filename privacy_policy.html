Health and Fitness Data Access and Use

Move Reminder accesses health and fitness data—specifically your step count and physical activity data—through Android Health Connect and/or your device’s built-in motion sensors.

  • Purpose of Access: This health data is accessed solely to track your hourly step progress and trigger local inactivity reminders when you have not reached your movement goal.
  • Local Processing Only: All step count data, reminder schedules, and app settings are processed and stored exclusively locally on your device.
  • No External Collection, Sale, or Sharing: Endura Software does not transmit, upload, collect, store, sell, or share your health data or personal information on any external servers or with any third parties (including advertising networks or data brokers).

Information Collection and Third-Party Services

Move Reminder does not require you to create an account, and Endura Software does not directly collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number).

However, the app uses Google Play Services, a third-party service provided by Google that may collect limited technical information used to identify your device or ensure app functionality. You can review Google’s privacy practices here:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy

Log Data

In the event of an app crash or error, standard diagnostic information (Log Data) may be collected automatically by the Android operating system or Google Play Services. This Log Data may include information such as your device’s Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device model, Android operating system version, app configuration at the time of the error, and the time and date of the event.

Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Because Move Reminder does not require user accounts and does not transmit or store your step data or personal information on external servers, Endura Software holds no remote user data to delete.

You retain full control over all data stored locally on your device and can delete it at any time using any of the following methods:

  • Clear App Data or Uninstall: You can permanently delete all locally stored step history and preferences by clearing Move Reminder’s storage in your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Move Reminder > Storage > Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.
  • Revoke Health Connect Permissions: You can revoke Move Reminder’s access to your step count data at any time through Android Health Connect (Settings > Security & privacy > Privacy > Health Connect > App permissions > Move Reminder).
  • Support Inquiries: If you have questions regarding data deletion or privacy, you may contact us at endurasoftware2018@gmail.com.
